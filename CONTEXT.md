# Course Registration

The language used to describe a customer's enrollment in a scheduled course.

## Language

**Course session**:
A scheduled occurrence of a course, with a date, time, location and limited seats.
_Avoid_: Checkout Session when referring to the class.

**Registration**:
A customer's enrollment in a course session.
_Avoid_: Payment when referring to enrollment.

**Registration outcome**:
The customer-facing account of whether enrollment is confirmed, unresolved or requires further action, considered alongside payment and refund progress.
_Avoid_: Payment status when referring to the overall registration outcome.

**Confirmed registration**:
An enrollment whose completion has been verified; receiving payment alone does not establish confirmation.
_Avoid_: Paid when referring to a reserved seat.

**Unavailable registration status**:
An outcome for which there is insufficient information to verify enrollment, even when payment details remain available. It does not establish that the enrollment failed or was cancelled.

**Receipt link**:
A protected link through which its holder can view payment details and the available registration outcome, including from another device.
